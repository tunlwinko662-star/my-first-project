Zardetkyi Island

Zadetkyi Island or Zadetkyi Kyun, also known as Saint Matthew's Island, is an island at the southern end of the Mergui Archipelago, Burma. Its northern side forms the southern shore of Hastings Harbor, where there is a base of the Myanmar Navy.

1. Diving & Snorkeling Paradise – The island is surrounded by colorful coral reefs and is home to a wide range of marine life, making it a perfect location for diving and snorkeling.
2. Secluded Beaches & Clear Waters – The island has soft white sand beaches and crystal-clear waters, ideal for relaxation and water activities.
3. Tropical Rainforests & Wildlife – Zardetkyi Island has dense jungle areas with rare bird species, butterflies, and small mammals, making it great for nature lovers and hikers.
4. Fishing & Kayaking – The waters around the island are perfect for kayaking, paddleboarding, and fishing, offering a peaceful experience in a natural setting.
5. Less Crowded & Peaceful – Unlike more popular islands, Zardetkyi remains largely untouched, making it a great destination for eco-tourism and private retreats.
