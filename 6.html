Salon Island

Salon Island is located in the southern part of Myanmar, within the Andaman Sea. Specifically, it is part of the Mergui Archipelago, which lies near Kawthaung.

The island is home to the Moken people, also known as “Sea Gypsies,” who have a unique nomadic lifestyle. Due to its natural beauty, Salon Island attracts travelers seeking pristine beaches, lush forests, and rich marine life. The area is also well known for snorkeling and diving, offering visitors a chance to explore vibrant coral reefs and diverse underwater ecosystems.
