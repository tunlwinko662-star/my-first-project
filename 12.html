Macleod Island

Macleod Island is one of the most beautiful and exclusive islands in the Mergui Archipelago, located in Kawthaung District, Tanintharyi Region, Myanmar. It is well-known for its eco-friendly resort, untouched natural beauty, and excellent diving spots.

1. Luxury Eco-Resort (Myanmar Andaman Resort) – Macleod Island is home to a sustainable eco-resort, offering a private island experience with beachfront bungalows and nature-based activities.
2. Snorkeling & Diving Paradise – The island is surrounded by coral reefs, making it one of the best spots in Myanmar for scuba diving and snorkeling.
3. Hiking & Wildlife Exploration – The island has jungle trekking trails, where visitors can explore dense tropical forests and encounter exotic birds, butterflies, and monkeys.
4. Secluded White Sand Beaches – With soft white sand and turquoise waters, Macleod Island offers a truly peaceful and relaxing escape.
5. Kayaking & Paddleboarding – The island’s calm bays are perfect for kayaking, paddleboarding, and exploring hidden coves
