Nyaung Wee Island(Buda Island)

Nyaung Wee Island is part of the Mergui Archipelago, located in Kawthaung District, Tanintharyi Region, Myanmar. It is one of the key islands where the Moken (Sea Gypsies) live, making it a fascinating place for cultural experiences, snorkeling, and eco-tourism.

1. Moken (Sea Gypsy) Villages – Nyaung Wee Island is home to indigenous Moken people, offering visitors a unique chance to learn about their traditional seafaring lifestyle, fishing techniques, and stilt houses.
2. Snorkeling & Diving – The island’s surrounding waters are filled with coral reefs, tropical fish, and even occasional sea turtles, making it a great place for underwater exploration.
3. Mangrove Forests & Rich Wildlife – The island features lush tropical greenery and mangrove forests, home to various bird species and marine creatures.
4. Quiet & Secluded Beaches – With its pristine white sand beaches and clear blue waters, Nyaung Wee Island is a perfect escape for relaxation.
5. Sustainable Eco-Tourism Destination – Since the island remains largely undeveloped, it is an ideal spot for eco-friendly tourism and experiencing nature in its purest form.
