Wa Ale Island

Wa Ale Island is located in the Mergui Archipelago, Kawthaung District, Tanintharyi Region, Myanmar. It lies within the protected area of Lampi Marine National Park, making it one of the most pristine and eco-friendly islands in the reg.

Luxury Eco-Resort (Wa Ale Resort) – The island is home to an exclusive eco-resort, which follows sustainable tourism practices. It is one of the most luxurious yet environmentally conscious resorts in Myanmar.
2. Untouched Beaches & Crystal-Clear Waters – The island offers white sandy beaches, hidden coves, and clear waters perfect for snorkeling and diving.
3. Lush Tropical Forests & Wildlife – As part of Lampi Marine National Park, the island is rich in biodiversity, including sea turtles, hornbills, and rare marine species.
4. Diving & Snorkeling – Visitors can explore coral reefs, caves, and diverse marine life.
5. Sea Turtle Conservation – The island supports a turtle conservation program, protecting species like the Green Turtle and Hawksbill Turtle..
