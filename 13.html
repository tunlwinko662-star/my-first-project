Bocho Island

Bocho Island is part of the Mergui Archipelago, a group of over 800 islands in the Andaman Sea, located off the southern coast of Myanmar. It is situated in Kawthaung District, Tanintharyi Region, near the Thai border.

Bocho Island is surrounded by the Andaman Sea and features white sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters, and dense tropical forests. •It is close to Lampi Island, which is a designated marine national park, making it a key spot for snorkeling, diving, and ecotourism. The island is relatively undeveloped, maintaining its natural beauty and rich biodiversity.
