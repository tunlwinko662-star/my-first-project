Smart Island

Smart Island is located within the Mergui Archipelago in Myanmar. It appears to be near MacLeod Island, which is part of the Mergui Archipelago in Tanintharyi Region, southern Myanmar.

Smart Island is a hidden gem in Myanmar’s Mergui Archipelago, known for its rocky shoreline, crystal-clear waters, and eco-friendly tourism. Unlike typical sandy beaches, its coast is covered with smooth black pebbles, creating a unique and picturesque landscape. The island is surrounded by a marine conservation zone, making it an excellent spot for snorkeling, scuba diving, and kayaking, with thriving coral reefs and diverse marine life. To preserve its natural beauty, Smart Island promotes sustainable tourism, with limited eco-friendly resorts that use solar power and rainwater collection. Visitors can enjoy island hopping, beach camping, and wildlife exploration, making it an ideal destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers..
