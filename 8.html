115 island

115 Island (Bo Ywe Island) is located in the Mergui Archipelago, southern Myanmar, within Tanintharyi Region. It is situated in the Andaman Sea, southwest of Kawthaung, and is one of the many pristine islands in the archipelago.

115 island is famous for its white sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters, coral reefs, and rich marine biodiversity. It is a popular spot for snorkeling, diving, kayaking, and beach camping. The surrounding waters are home to colorful fish, sea turtles, and coral gardens, making it an ideal destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers. To get to 115 Island, travelers must first go to Kawthaung (the southernmost town of Myanmar) and then take a speedboat or yacht, which takes around 2-3 hours depending on weather conditions. Many tour operators include 115 Island as part of island-hopping tours in the Mergui Archipelago.
