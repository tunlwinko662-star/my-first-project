Than Kyun Island

Than Kyun, also known as Richie’s Island, is located in the Mergui Archipelago, Kawthaung District, Tanintharyi Region, Myanmar. It is one of the southernmost islands in the archipelago and is close to Kawthaung and the Thai border.

1. Close to Kawthaung – Than Kyun is one of the easiest islands to reach, making it a great choice for a short island getaway.
2. Lush Tropical Forests – The island is covered in dense greenery, home to various bird species and wildlife.
3. Rich Marine Biodiversity – The surrounding waters are ideal for snorkeling and fishing, with colorful coral reefs and abundant fish species.
4. Tranquil Beaches – Than Kyun offers quiet and unspoiled beaches, perfect for relaxation away from tourist crowds.
5. Local Fishing Villages – Visitors can see traditional Myanmar fishing communities, offering a cultural experience.
