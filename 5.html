Lampi Island

Lampi Island is located in the Mergui Archipelago, Tanintharyi Region, Myanmar, within the Andaman Sea. It is Myanmar’s first Marine National Park, established to protect its rich biodiversity and pristine ecosystems.

The island is home to dense mangrove forests, diverse marine life, and rare wildlife species. It serves as an important habitat for sea turtles, dolphins, and various bird species. With its crystal-clear waters and unspoiled natural beauty, Lampi Island is a significant site for eco-tourism, conservation efforts, and sustainable development. Due to its ecological importance, efforts are being made to preserve the island’s forests, marine environment, and local communities while promoting responsible tourism and environmental awareness.
