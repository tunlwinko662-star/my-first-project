ThaHtay Island

Thahtay Island is part of the Mergui Archipelago, located in Kawthaung District, Tanintharyi Region, Myanmar. The island is known for its pristine beaches, clear waters, and abundant marine life, making it a great spot for snorkeling and diving. It is also close to the mainland, making it easily accessible.

1. Easily Accessible from Kawthaung – Thahtay Island is one of the closest islands to the mainland, making it a convenient island getaway.
2. Snorkeling & Diving Hotspot – The waters around the island have colorful coral reefs and diverse marine species, perfect for snorkeling and scuba diving.
3. White Sandy Beaches & Crystal-Clear Waters – The island offers pristine beaches with soft white sand and stunning blue-green waters.
4. Dense Greenery & Wildlife – The island is covered in tropical forests, providing a home for various bird species and small animals.
5. Peaceful & Uncrowded – Unlike more developed tourist destinations, Thahtay Island remains secluded and natural, making it an excellent place for relaxation.
