Lanbi Island

Christie Island is one of the southernmost islands in the Mergui Archipelago, located in Kawthaung District, Tanintharyi Region, Myanmar. It is famous for its stunning coral reefs, rich marine biodiversity, and crystal-clear waters, making it a top destination for scuba diving and snorkeling.

1. One of Myanmar’s Best Dive Sites – Christie Island is known for its spectacular dive sites, with deep waters full of sharks, rays, and large schools of fish.
2. Untouched Coral Reefs – The island has some of the most pristine coral reefs in the region, perfect for snorkeling and underwater photography.
3. Remote & Uninhabited – Christie Island is completely uninhabited, making it a great destination for adventure travelers and eco-tourists.
4. Abundant Marine Life – Divers can spot whale sharks, manta rays, barracudas, and sea turtles around the island’s deep waters.
5. Secluded White Sand Beaches – The island offers pristine, quiet beaches where visitors can relax after a dive or snorkel.
